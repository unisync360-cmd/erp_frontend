These exams are stored in this browser and are read by the matching Student Online Exam page.

How this demo works

1. Faculty creates questions and publishes an exam.

2. Student opens Online Exams in the Student Dashboard.

3. The student selects the published exam, enters details, writes it and submits.

4. The score and answer review appear after submission.

Important: This is a front-end demonstration using browser localStorage. To share exams between different faculty/student devices or accounts, connect a common server/database and add authentication.
